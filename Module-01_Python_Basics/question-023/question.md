23.	Convergence Simulator
Simulate a sequence defined by xk+1=0.5xk+1x_{k+1} = 0.5 x_k + 1xk+1=0.5xk+1 starting from a user-provided x0. Iterate until the absolute difference between successive terms is below a given tolerance or a maximum number of iterations is reached. Print all terms and the final converged value.
