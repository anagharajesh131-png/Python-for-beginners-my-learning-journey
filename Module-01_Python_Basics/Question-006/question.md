Compound Measurement Calculator
A measurement involves three quantities: voltage (V), current (I), and time (t). Read these from the user, then compute and print:
o	Power P=V×IP = V \times IP=V×I
o	Energy E=P×tE = P \times tE=P×t
o	Energy in watt-hours and kilowatt-hours
Format all outputs neatly with labels and consistent decimal places.
